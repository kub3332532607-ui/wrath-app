FONT LICENSES / 字體授權聲明

The web fonts in this folder (body.woff2, title.woff2, logo.woff2, jp.woff2) are
small SUBSETS (only the characters this site needs, converted to WOFF2) cut from
the open-source fonts listed below. All of them are licensed under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1, reproduced in full at the end of this file. They are
not sold by themselves.

只留本站用得到的字，並轉成 WOFF2。全部採 SIL Open Font License 1.1（全文附在
本檔最後），不單獨販售。
